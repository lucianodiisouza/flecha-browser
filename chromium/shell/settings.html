Each Space keeps its own colour; in dark mode it becomes a deep tint of that colour.

Drag tabs and folders to reorder them or move them between Spaces. ⌘-click or ⇧-click selects several; ↩ edits, ⌫ deletes. Deleted tabs stay findable in the Archive.

To keep a Space's cookies and logins apart from the others, open its editor (double-click the Space name) and turn on Separate cookies and logins.

Boosts are your own TypeScript and CSS for any site, kept in Flecha and edited on the Boosts page. They're named after the host: github.com.ts, github.com.css, or all.css for every site. Styles apply as you save; scripts reload the page.
